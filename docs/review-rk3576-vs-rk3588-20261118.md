## Source inconsistencies and limits

- RK3576 older full datasheet, product page and newer brief do not present video maxima uniformly. The article explains codec/profile/revision dependence instead of combining maxima into a single 8K claim.
- Sige5's top specification and shared Sige-family instructions do not consistently state a DSI maximum. The comparison lists the actual display interface families and requires panel/mode validation; no disputed DSI resolution is asserted.
- CM5 documentation lists V2.0 memory while an introductory note describes its launch in future tense. The article describes revision differences, not current stock or shipment availability.
- Shared SBC dimensions do not establish mechanical or pin compatibility.
- No new hardware benchmark, power measurement, sensor validation, or model conversion was performed. Commands are syntax-checked diagnostic examples for Linux; permissions and utility availability vary by image.
